# Northfield General Hospital - Billing FAQ

## When will I get my bill?
Most bills are issued within 30 days of discharge. We bill your
insurance first; you receive a statement after your insurer responds.

## Can I get an itemized bill?
Yes. Request one at any time from Patient Financial Services; it is
free and provided within 7 business days.

## I can't afford my bill. What are my options?
Northfield offers zero-interest payment plans from 6 to 24 months and
a financial assistance program. Households earning up to 300% of the
federal poverty level may qualify for partial or full assistance.
Apply through Patient Financial Services. Emergency care is never
delayed over ability to pay.

## Do you offer a prompt-pay discount?
Yes: 15% off the patient-responsibility portion when paid in full
within 30 days of the first statement.

## Insurance coverage questions
Whether a service is covered depends on your plan. Call the number on
your insurance card, or ask us for the billing codes so your insurer
can confirm coverage.
