# Northfield General Hospital - Visiting Hours Policy

Policy NGH-PS-101. Effective 2026-01-01.

## General inpatient wards
Visitors are welcome daily from 9:00 am to 8:00 pm. Two visitors may
be at the bedside at a time; others may wait in the family lounge on
each floor.

## Intensive Care Unit (ICU)
ICU visiting hours are 10:00 am to 8:00 pm daily, two visitors at a
time, immediate family preferred. Children under 12 require clearance
from the charge nurse before visiting the ICU.

## Maternity (Family Birth Center)
Partners and support persons are welcome 24 hours a day. Other
visitors, including siblings of the newborn: 10:00 am to 8:00 pm.

## Emergency Department
One support person per patient in treatment areas, at the care team's
discretion during procedures.

## Overnight stays
One adult family member may stay overnight with a patient in a private
room with nursing approval. Overnight guests are not permitted in the
ICU.

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours are 2:00 pm to 4:00 pm daily. Visits are permitted during
quiet hours; please keep noise to a minimum.
