# Northfield General Hospital - Discharge Process

Policy NGH-CL-310.

## The day before discharge
Your care team confirms your going-home plan: medications, follow-up
appointments, and any home equipment. Ask questions early - the day
of discharge is busy.

## On the day of discharge
1. Your attending physician writes the discharge order, usually
   before 11:00 am.
2. Your nurse reviews the discharge summary with you, including
   medications and warning signs.
3. New prescriptions can be filled at the Building A pharmacy before
   you leave.
4. Patients are escorted to the main entrance; your ride should wait
   in the 15-minute pickup zone.

## After you leave
- Your discharge summary is sent to your primary care provider within
  48 hours.
- A care coordinator calls within 2 business days to check on you.
- Follow-up appointments on your summary are confirmed by text
  message.

## If you feel worse at home
Call the nurse advice line at 555-0142, any time. For emergencies -
chest pain, trouble breathing, severe bleeding, stroke symptoms -
call 911.
